# Support Center Handbook

The support team is available Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 Jordan time. Messages received outside these hours are queued for the next working day.

Customers can change a subscription plan from the billing page. A downgrade takes effect at the next renewal date, while an upgrade is applied immediately and charges the prorated difference.

Refund requests must be submitted within fourteen days of the original charge. The billing team reviews the request and returns approved refunds to the original payment method within five to ten business days.

For account access issues, the customer must verify the account email before an agent can change profile details. Never ask a customer to share a password, one-time code, or full payment card number.

Critical incidents should be escalated when multiple customers cannot access the product, a payment is duplicated, or personal data may have been exposed. Include the account identifier, timestamp, impact, and steps already attempted.
