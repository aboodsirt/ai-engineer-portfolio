# Support Handbook

## Escalation policy

Escalate a customer issue when the request involves account security, a payment dispute, a service outage, or a repeated failure after two troubleshooting attempts. Include the customer identifier, timeline, steps already tried, and the desired outcome.

## Response targets

Priority one incidents receive an initial response within thirty minutes. Priority two incidents receive an initial response within four business hours. Priority three requests receive an initial response within two business days.

## Refund policy

Monthly subscriptions can be refunded within fourteen days of the original charge when the account has not exceeded the fair-use threshold. Annual subscriptions are reviewed by the billing team on a case-by-case basis.
